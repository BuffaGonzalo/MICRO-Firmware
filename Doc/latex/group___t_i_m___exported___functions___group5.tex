\begin{DoxyNote}{Nota}
Switching from Center Aligned counter mode to Edge counter mode (or reverse) requires a timer reset to avoid unexpected direction due to DIR bit readonly in center aligned mode. Ex: call \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group5_gae60b468b11199522c6c83a943439c7b7}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+One\+Pulse\+\_\+\+De\+Init()} before \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group5_ga476d67a220c23ebdc69fac7b09dbaa72}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+One\+Pulse\+\_\+\+Init()} 

When the timer instance is initialized in One Pulse mode, timer channels 1 and channel 2 are reserved and cannot be used for other purpose. 
\end{DoxyNote}
