\begin{DoxyNote}{Nota}
Switching from Center Aligned counter mode to Edge counter mode (or reverse) requires a timer reset to avoid unexpected direction due to DIR bit readonly in center aligned mode. Ex: call \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group6_gaaf99281fd7635e20c08e48bfc9ea11e3}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+Encoder\+\_\+\+De\+Init()} before \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group6_gabbd8d70ab36161f83e1c09087789955d}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+Encoder\+\_\+\+Init()} 

Encoder mode and External clock mode 2 are not compatible and must not be selected together Ex: A call for \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group6_gabbd8d70ab36161f83e1c09087789955d}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+Encoder\+\_\+\+Init} will erase the settings of \doxylink{group___t_i_m___exported___functions___group8_gaa29ed013caefca2a181a1cabac66d521}{HAL\+\_\+\+TIM\+\_\+\+Config\+Clock\+Source} using TIM\+\_\+\+CLOCKSOURCE\+\_\+\+ETRMODE2 and vice versa 

When the timer instance is initialized in Encoder mode, timer channels 1 and channel 2 are reserved and cannot be used for other purpose. 
\end{DoxyNote}
